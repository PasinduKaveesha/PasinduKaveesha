## Who Am I

A DevOps-focused engineer with a solid backend foundation, building infrastructure skills by actually building infrastructure — servers, pipelines, and automation, not just theory.

My core strength is in Linux, Docker, Git, Bash, Java, and Node.js — solid at an intermediate level and growing daily. Everything past that (cloud, orchestration, IaC) is deliberate, active learning, not a finished skillset.

I care about systems that are reproducible, automated, and boring in the best way — because boring means reliable.
